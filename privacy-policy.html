Privacy Policy - Stanislav AI Booking

Last updated: 2026-09-13

Stanislav AI Booking handles barbershop booking conversations through Instagram.

Information we process

We may process the Instagram user ID, message text, and appointment details provided in a booking conversation.

How information is used

Information is used to answer booking questions, check availability, create or update appointments, and send replies through Instagram. We do not sell personal information or use conversation data for advertising.

Service providers

The service uses Meta/Instagram APIs, n8n, Google Calendar, and an AI processing provider. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and security

API credentials are stored in protected service credentials and are not included in source code. Conversation and appointment data are retained only as needed to provide the booking service and maintain the connected calendar.

Requests

For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact @stanislav.n8n.ai.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the service or its integrations change.
