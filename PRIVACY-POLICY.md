# Privacy Policy — Stanislav AI Booking

Last updated: 2026-09-13

Stanislav AI Booking is a demonstration and automation service for handling
barbershop booking conversations through Instagram.

## Information we process

When a person contacts the connected Instagram account, the service may
process:

- the Instagram user ID supplied by Meta;
- the message text and booking details provided by the person;
- appointment details needed to check availability and create a booking.

## How information is used

Information is used only to:

- respond to booking questions;
- check appointment availability;
- create, update, or cancel an appointment when requested;
- send the requested reply through Instagram.

We do not sell personal information or use conversation data for advertising.

## Service providers

The service uses Meta/Instagram APIs, n8n, Google Calendar, and an AI
processing provider to deliver the requested booking functionality. Each
provider processes data according to its own terms and privacy policy.

## Retention and security

Access tokens and API credentials are stored in protected service credentials
and are not included in application source code. Conversation and appointment
data are retained only as long as needed to provide the booking service and
maintain the connected calendar.

## User requests

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated
with an Instagram conversation, contact the account that received the
conversation at [@stanislav.n8n.ai](https://www.instagram.com/stanislav.n8n.ai/).

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the service or its integrations change. The
effective date above identifies the latest version.
